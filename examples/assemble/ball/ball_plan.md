# Build plan: ball

3 parts, 2 fasteners, 3 steps, 2 flips.

## Before you start

### Bill of materials

- 2 × M5x20 socket head screw
- 2 × M5 hex nut
- 1 × core (printed)
- 2 × shell (printed)

### Preload sheet (nuts)

- **core**: 2 hex nuts — M5 into the pocket on the bot face for the bak screw (shell#2); M5 into the pocket on the top face for the rt screw (shell#1).

## Steps

### Step 1 — left (-X) face down
- Place: core
- Preload: core — 2 × M5 hex nuts into the pockets on the top face, on the bot face

### Step 2 — top (+Z) face down (flip the assembly)
- Place: shell#2 (on core)
- Tighten: M5x20 socket head through shell#2 into the hex nut in core (bak face) — 0.6 Nm (printed part: finger tight + ¼ turn, no power driver)
- Note: driver axis is not vertical here (bak face of core); keep the driver square to the bracket

### Step 3 — left (-X) face down (flip the assembly)
- Place: shell#1 (on core)
- Tighten: M5x20 socket head through shell#1 into the hex nut in core (rt face) — 0.6 Nm (printed part: finger tight + ¼ turn, no power driver)
