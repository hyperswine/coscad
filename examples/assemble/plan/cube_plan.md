# Build plan: cube

29 parts, 38 fasteners, 29 steps, 4 flips.

## Before you start

### Bill of materials

- 38 × M5x10 button head screw
- 38 × M5 drop-in T-nut
- 16 × br (printed)
- 1 × px (printed)
- 4 × rx (aluminium)
- 4 × ry (aluminium)
- 4 × rz (printed)

### Preload sheet (T-nuts)

- **rx#1**: 4 nuts — bot slot: 170, 30; fwd slot: 170, 30. Mark the -X (left) end; positions are mm from the mark.
- **rx#2**: 4 nuts — bak slot: 170, 30; bot slot: 170, 30. Mark the -X (left) end; positions are mm from the mark.
- **rx#3**: 4 nuts — fwd slot: 170, 30; top slot: 170, 30. Mark the -X (left) end; positions are mm from the mark.
- **rx#4**: 4 nuts — bak slot: 170, 30; top slot: 170, 30. Mark the -X (left) end; positions are mm from the mark.
- **ry#1**: 2 nuts — bot slot: 150, 10. Mark the -Y (front) end; positions are mm from the mark.
- **ry#2**: 4 nuts — bot slot: 150, 10; rt slot: 120, 40. Mark the -Y (front) end; positions are mm from the mark.
- **ry#3**: 2 nuts — top slot: 150, 10. Mark the -Y (front) end; positions are mm from the mark.
- **ry#4**: 4 nuts — rt slot: 120, 40; top slot: 150, 10. Mark the -Y (front) end; positions are mm from the mark.
- **rz#1**: 2 nuts — fwd slot: 150, 10. Mark the -Z (bottom) end; positions are mm from the mark.
- **rz#2**: 3 nuts — fwd slot: 150, 10; rt slot: 80. Mark the -Z (bottom) end; positions are mm from the mark.
- **rz#3**: 2 nuts — bak slot: 150, 10. Mark the -Z (bottom) end; positions are mm from the mark.
- **rz#4**: 3 nuts — bak slot: 150, 10; rt slot: 80. Mark the -Z (bottom) end; positions are mm from the mark.
