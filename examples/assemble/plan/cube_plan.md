### Preload sheet (nuts)

- **bottom_back**: 4 nuts — bak slot: 170, 30; bot slot: 170, 30. Mark the -X (left) end; positions are mm from the mark.
- **bottom_front**: 4 nuts — bot slot: 170, 30; fwd slot: 170, 30. Mark the -X (left) end; positions are mm from the mark.
- **bottom_left**: 2 nuts — bot slot: 150, 10. Mark the -Y (front) end; positions are mm from the mark.
- **bottom_right**: 4 nuts — bot slot: 150, 10; rt slot: 120, 40. Mark the -Y (front) end; positions are mm from the mark.
- **top_back**: 4 nuts — bak slot: 170, 30; top slot: 170, 30. Mark the -X (left) end; positions are mm from the mark.
- **top_front**: 4 nuts — fwd slot: 170, 30; top slot: 170, 30. Mark the -X (left) end; positions are mm from the mark.
- **top_left**: 2 nuts — top slot: 150, 10. Mark the -Y (front) end; positions are mm from the mark.
- **top_right**: 4 nuts — rt slot: 120, 40; top slot: 150, 10. Mark the -Y (front) end; positions are mm from the mark.

- **upright_fl**: 2 nuts — fwd slot: 150, 10. Mark the -Z (bottom) end; positions are mm from the mark.
- **upright_fr**: 3 nuts — fwd slot: 150, 10; rt slot: 80. Mark the -Z (bottom) end; positions are mm from the mark.

## Steps

### Step 1 — back (+Y) face down
- Place: bottom_front
- Preload: bottom_front — 4 × M5 drop-in nuts (bot @ 30, bot @ 170, fwd @ 30, fwd @ 170)

### Step 2 — back (+Y) face down
- Place: top_front
- Preload: top_front — 4 × M5 drop-in nuts (top @ 30, top @ 170, fwd @ 30, fwd @ 170)

### Step 3 — back (+Y) face down
- Place: upright_fl
- Preload: upright_fl — 2 × M5 drop-in nuts (fwd @ 10, fwd @ 150)

### Step 4 — back (+Y) face down
- Place: upright_fr
- Preload: upright_fr — 3 × M5 drop-in nuts (fwd @ 10, fwd @ 150, rt @ 80)

### Step 5 — back (+Y) face down
- Place: br_front_low_right (on bottom_front, upright_fr)
- Tighten: M5x10 button head into drop-in T-nut on bottom_front fwd slot at 170 mm — 3.0 Nm
- Tighten: M5x10 button head into drop-in T-nut on upright_fr fwd slot at 10 mm — 0.6 Nm (printed part: finger tight + ¼ turn, no power driver)

### Step 6 — back (+Y) face down
- Place: br_front_low_left (on bottom_front, upright_fl)
- Tighten: M5x10 button head into drop-in T-nut on bottom_front fwd slot at 30 mm — 3.0 Nm
- Tighten: M5x10 button head into drop-in T-nut on upright_fl fwd slot at 10 mm — 0.6 Nm (printed part: finger tight + ¼ turn, no power driver)

### Step 7 — back (+Y) face down
- Place: br_front_high_right (on top_front, upright_fr)
- Tighten: M5x10 button head into drop-in T-nut on top_front fwd slot at 170 mm — 3.0 Nm
- Tighten: M5x10 button head into drop-in T-nut on upright_fr fwd slot at 150 mm — 0.6 Nm (printed part: finger tight + ¼ turn, no power driver)

### Step 8 — back (+Y) face down

### Step 9 — bottom (-Z) face down (flip the assembly)

### Step 10 — bottom (-Z) face down
