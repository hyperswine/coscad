# Build plan: corner

5 parts, 6 fasteners, 4 steps, 1 flips.

## Before you start

### Bill of materials

- 6 × M5x10 socket head screw
- 6 × M5 drop-in T-nut
- 1 × bracket1 (printed)
- 1 × bracket2 (printed)
- 1 × inner (printed)
- 1 × railH (aluminium)
- 1 × railV (aluminium)

### Preload sheet (nuts)

- **railH**: 3 nuts — @foot_inner slot: 20; @outer_bore_bracket1 slot: 12; @outer_bore_bracket2 slot: 12. Mark the -X (left) end; positions are mm from the mark.
- **railV**: 3 nuts — @outer_bracket1 slot: 12; @outer_bracket2 slot: 12; @side_inner slot: 20. Mark the -Z (bottom) end; positions are mm from the mark.

## Steps

### Step 1 — front (-Y) face down
- Preload: railH — 3 × M5 drop-in nuts (@foot_inner @ 20, @outer_bore_bracket1 @ 12, @outer_bore_bracket2 @ 12)
- Preload: railV — 3 × M5 drop-in nuts (@side_inner @ 20, @outer_bracket1 @ 12, @outer_bracket2 @ 12)
- Note: Load the nuts into railH in hand, turning it to reach the pockets or slots; retain them while placing the part
- Note: Load the nuts into railV in hand, turning it to reach the pockets or slots; retain them while placing the part

### Step 2 — front (-Y) face down
- Place: railH
- Place: railV
- Place: bracket1 (on railH, railV)
- Tighten: M5x10 socket head into drop-in T-nut on railH @outer_bore_bracket1 slot at 12 mm — 3.0 Nm
- Tighten: M5x10 socket head into drop-in T-nut on railV @outer_bracket1 slot at 12 mm — 3.0 Nm

### Step 3 — front (-Y) face down
- Place: inner (on railH, railV)
- Tighten: M5x10 socket head into drop-in T-nut on railH @foot_inner slot at 20 mm — 3.0 Nm
- Tighten: M5x10 socket head into drop-in T-nut on railV @side_inner slot at 20 mm — 3.0 Nm
- Note: driver axis is not vertical here (@foot_inner face of railH); keep the driver square to the bracket
- Note: driver axis is not vertical here (@side_inner face of railV); keep the driver square to the bracket

### Step 4 — bottom (-Z) face down (flip the assembly)
- Place: bracket2 (on railH, railV)
- Tighten: M5x10 socket head into drop-in T-nut on railH @outer_bore_bracket2 slot at 12 mm — 3.0 Nm
- Tighten: M5x10 socket head into drop-in T-nut on railV @outer_bracket2 slot at 12 mm — 3.0 Nm
- Note: railV is not lying flat in this step; hold its T-nut with a finger while starting the screw
- Note: driver axis is not vertical here (@outer_bore_bracket2 face of railH); keep the driver square to the bracket
- Note: driver axis is not vertical here (@outer_bracket2 face of railV); keep the driver square to the bracket
