# Build plan: tesseract

29 parts, 40 fasteners, 28 steps, 1 flips.

## Before you start

### Bill of materials

- 24 × M3x16 socket head screw
- 24 × M3 hex nut
- 12 × bar (printed)
- 8 × corner (printed)
- 1 × inner (printed)
- 8 × strut (printed)

### Preload sheet (nuts)

- **corner#1**: 3 hex nuts — M3 into the pocket on the bak face for the screw into bar#9; M3 into the pocket on the rt face for the screw into bar#5; M3 into the pocket on the top face for the screw into bar#1.
- **corner#2**: 3 hex nuts — M3 into the pocket on the lft face for the screw into bar#10; M3 into the pocket on the bak face for the screw into bar#1; M3 into the pocket on the top face for the screw into bar#6.
- **corner#3**: 3 hex nuts — M3 into the pocket on the fwd face for the screw into bar#11; M3 into the pocket on the lft face for the screw into bar#6; M3 into the pocket on the top face for the screw into bar#2.
- **corner#4**: 3 hex nuts — M3 into the pocket on the rt face for the screw into bar#12; M3 into the pocket on the fwd face for the screw into bar#2; M3 into the pocket on the top face for the screw into bar#5.
- **corner#5**: 3 hex nuts — M3 into the pocket on the rt face for the screw into bar#9; M3 into the pocket on the bak face for the screw into bar#3; M3 into the pocket on the bot face for the screw into bar#7.
- **corner#6**: 3 hex nuts — M3 into the pocket on the bak face for the screw into bar#10; M3 into the pocket on the lft face for the screw into bar#8; M3 into the pocket on the bot face for the screw into bar#3.
- **corner#7**: 3 hex nuts — M3 into the pocket on the lft face for the screw into bar#11; M3 into the pocket on the fwd face for the screw into bar#4; M3 into the pocket on the bot face for the screw into bar#8.
