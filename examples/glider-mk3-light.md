# One-piece Mk3 light

The 120 mm Mk3 has three enclosed cells per wing and three longitudinal
fuselage cavities. The cell boundaries leave ribs and a continuous trailing
edge. The solid forward fuselage provides the forward mass moment without
a ballast pocket. A tapered keel grip gives a handhold under the wing.
The wing moved aft 2.10 mm and the tail is set at −4° incidence.

At 1.26 g/cm³ the CAD material estimate is 10.60 g, versus 12.66 g for the
original empty Mk3. CG is y=+5.16 mm in the source coordinate system.
Using a triangular planform (25.87 cm², 29.81 mm MAC), the wing and tail
quarter-chord stations are y=+3.42 and +56.55 mm. A linear tail model
with area ratio 0.26, lift slope ratio 0.8, efficiency 0.8 and downwash
gradient 0.35 estimates a neutral point at y=+8.60 mm and 11.6% margin.
At CL=0.6 and sea-level density the same lift model gives 10.46 m/s
versus 11.43 m/s for the original empty CAD mass. At unchanged L/D this
would reduce sink by about 8.5%. The actual polar and glide duration
are unmeasured. The tail setting assumes a near-zero section pitching
moment and is sensitive to downwash at this span.

The STL was generated by the native CoScad backend. Sliced CG, support
removal at the grip and continuity of the closed wing skins require
verification before a print trial.
