# Examples index

- `examples/` — small single-part `.coscad` files (shapes, booleans,
  transforms, bezier, zoo of all primitives, and `loft` — numeric
  bindings plus lofted funnel and lug).
- `examples/topological/` — pipeline syntax and anchor-relative
  modelling: `pipeline_basics`, `topo_tour` (annotated), `tbracket`
  (the zero-translation seam bracket, verified identical to its
  coordinate-built twin).
- `examples/assemble/` — `.assemble` specs: `corner`/`frame`
  (recursion; `rail200`/`rail120` are plain-bar stand-ins for the
  2020 extrusions, referenced ×0), `spill` (multi-bed: the design stage
  reports plate overflow, `coscad next` spills to more beds), `autotest`
  (orientation search), and `bow3/` — the flagship three-part
  manufactured assembly.
- `examples/assemble/tesseract/` — a 100 mm nested-cube display with 12 mm
  split hubs and captured printed joints, no screws or nuts. The supported
  build, finished object, three-piece fit test and retained bolted version
  are described in [Tesseract](2026-10-13-TESSERACT.md).
- `examples-next/` — earlier manufacturing-era parts (bow2 generation,
  greaser tool with its manifest kept as a reference output), and
  `example_check.assemble` with parts `a`/`b`/`c` — a `coscad check`
  demo whose lid sits 0.2 mm above the blocks, under the declared 0.3 mm
  clearance.
- `examples/archive/` — a few historical scratch files (the old `debug*` and
  `test_*` files were removed in the repo-hygiene change).
- `examples/haskell/` — embedded-DSL samples for ghci (`cabal repl`, then `:load`
  examples/haskell/ExampleGear.hs`). They are not library code, but the
  `haskell-examples` test-suite compiles them and renders one shape from
  each, so they cannot silently rot.

Generated output is gitignored and not committed: `.scad`/`.stl`, manifests,
`_design.json`, plan `_plan.json`/`_plan.md` and `_step*` scenes and images.
Regenerate with `coscad <file>`, `coscad next <file>` or
`coscad plan [--png] <file>`. Exceptions that stay committed: the print
records under `models/` (each `next` manifest, the `_next_` part scenes and
`print-run.json`/`validation.json`), the tesseract adhesion-retry
`verification.json`, and the greaser reference manifest. Every example (outside
`archive/`) is compiled, rendered, and compared against
`test/golden/` by `cabal test`; see README "Tests".
