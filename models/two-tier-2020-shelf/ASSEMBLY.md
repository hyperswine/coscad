# Assembly sequence

Complete the nut/bolt/support-removal coupon first. The supplied nuts slide in
from a rail end: preload them **before** a rail end is blocked by another part.
Keep bolts only finger-tight until the frame is square and all six feet rest flat.

## Nut preload table

Positions are measured from the low end along each rail, in millimetres.
For an upright, the low end is its foot. Width-rail positions run left to right;
depth-rail positions run front to back.

| Member | Slot face | Nut positions | Nuts per member |
|---|---|---|---:|
| Each 230 mm post | Outward front/back face | 15, 35, 175, 195 | 4 |
| Each 230 mm post | Side face used by depth-rail gussets | 15, 35, 175, 195 | 4 |
| Each 170 mm width rail | Outward front/back face | 15, 35, 135, 155 | 4 |
| Each 170 mm width rail | Top | 30, 130 | 2 |
| Each 160 mm depth rail | Side face used by gussets | 15, 35, 125, 145 | 4 |

The left posts/depth rails use their left face, the right ones their right face,
and the centre ones their right face. The unused two faces remain available.
Total: 48 post nuts + 48 width-rail nuts + 24 depth-rail nuts = **120**.

## Build from the lower tier upward

1. Clean all T-slots, bolt holes, recessed seats and rail ends. Match left and
   right panels by their post notches. Rail ends meet squarely: remove elephant
   foot rather than forcing an oversized end into the frame.
2. Preload the post and lower-rail nuts. Loosely start bolts through gussets into
   the nuts where that helps retain them. The assembly coordinates are six post
   centres at x = -190, 0, 190 and y = -90, 90 mm.
3. Join the lower front and back 170 mm width rails to the posts, with rail
   centres 60 mm above the feet. Use L plates at the outer posts and T plates at
   the centre posts. All vertical gusset arms point downward.
4. Join the three 160 mm depth rails at the same height. Each end uses an L
   gusset: outside the left/right frame, and on the right face of the centre
   rail. The relieved gusset centre goes around the crossing width rail.
5. Square and level the lower frame. Lower the two panels over the post notches
   with ribs down. Put M5 × 12 button-head bolts into the recessed holes and
   preloaded top-slot nuts. The seam sits over the central depth rail.
6. Only after the lower panels are installed, add the upper width/depth rails
   at 220 mm centre height. Preload their panel nuts first. Use the same L/T
   arrangement, again with vertical arms downward.
7. Install the upper panels, ribs down. Recheck square, seam alignment and six
   feet contacting the tabletop. Snug bolts gradually with a hand driver; do
   not use an impact driver or assume metal-extrusion tightening torque.
8. Push gently from each side to check for racking, then perform the progressive
   distributed load test described in the README. Recheck after sustained load.

No glue, heat-set inserts, M3 fasteners or separate metal bracing are required.
The bolts hold the printed joints; a nut fit that damages the lip is a failed
fit test, not something to fix by tightening harder.
