# General-purpose clamp

The owner reported that the original two-piece print worked well. The printed screw thread ran well and the clamp developed decent clamping force by hand without extra equipment. This was a qualitative test.

The rail-body revision reuses the original thumbscrew. Slide a 2020 T-nut into the open end of either exterior track before fitting an accessory. The top track runs along the fixed jaw and the middle track runs along the outside of the spine. Both use the shelf project's loose-fit M5 sliding-nut profile. The new mounting lips have not been physically load-tested.
