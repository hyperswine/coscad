# Captive spring-return fit demonstrator

Print plate.stl as laid out in standard PLA. All five parts sit flat. A starting setup is a 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.20 mm layers, and thin-wall detection. Preview the spiral for a continuous extrusion before printing.

Seat the spring hub on the central post and its anchor eye on the small offset post. Fit the pinion on the D-shaped hub above the spring. Place the toothed carriage on the raised track with the two guide posts in its closed slots. Start with both posts centred and mesh the teeth. Press the keeper onto the posts just far enough to retain the carriage while leaving it free to slide. Operate flat on the bench.

Move the carriage gently 1–2 mm from centre and guide it back. Check separately for sliding friction, pinion rubbing, and spring recovery. The slots allow approximately 6.6 mm total travel, about 3.3 mm either way from centre. The 1½ turns describe the shape of the strip at rest. No multi-turn winding capacity is established. Keeper interference and keyed fit are nominal and untested.

Physical result, 15 November 2026: the owner reported that the print worked. The two-hole keeper was difficult to fit and the pinion alignment felt flimsy.
