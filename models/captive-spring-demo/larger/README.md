# Larger captive spring-return prototype

The first print worked. This revision increases the footprint by 50% in each horizontal axis and keeps the original spring height. The strip is 0.90 mm wide. Carriage travel is about 9.9 mm end to end.

Place the rotor, gear and carriage as on the first print. The new gear's raised circular collar faces upwards. Lower the bridge keeper over the two carriage posts and the gear collar together. Seat the rear end on its pedestal. Its underside should sit about 0.35 mm above the gear face. The two guide holes have 0.48 mm diametral clearance and the gear collar has 0.45 mm diametral clearance in the keeper. The rear locating peg is nominally line-to-line. Do not force the bridge or bow it down onto the gear.

With the base flat, move the carriage a few millimetres and release it gently. Check that the cover stays seated, the pinion stays level and the carriage returns without rubbing. Keeper fit and upper journal alignment are prototype checks.
