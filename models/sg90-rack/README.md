# SG90 rack and pinion

The deck prints working-face down. Turn it over to stand on the four legs.
Insert the servo from underneath, with its output shaft toward the rack and
centred on the gear axis. The aperture is 13.1 × 23.9 mm. Check mounting
ear alignment on your servo and use flush or underside fastener heads so
the pinion clears them. The printed shims are optional for adjusting height.

Fit the servo's straight horn into the 19 × 5.8 mm recess on the pinion,
with the recess facing the servo. Check for a snug drive fit with no play
and retain with the servo's centre screw or a matching longer screw if
needed. Horn sizes differ. Keep the pinion and rack teeth at the same height.

Place two M3 smooth shanks with washers through the closed rack slot and
guide holes. Retain with nuts, allowing the rack to move under the washers.
The slot ends bear on the shanks at nominal ±12 mm. Centre the servo and
rack with power off for assembly, then command a small sweep and increase
gradually. A starting limit of ±55° gives about 19 mm of travel. Leave the
hard stops as backups and avoid driving the servo into them.
