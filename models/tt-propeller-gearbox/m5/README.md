# M5 TT propeller gearbox

The five equal gears transfer the drive 132.8 mm at 1:1. Use ordinary M5 machine screws and M5 hex nuts at every fastened joint.

- **Cover:** four M5 × 20 mm screws and nuts. Seat the input journal through the floor, the three idlers on their posts and the output shaft through the cover. Turn the gears by hand as you tighten.
- **Motor:** seat the TT shaft fully in the driver. Position the printed bridge across the motor body and pass two M5 screws through the bridge and the slotted tongue. Select length from the assembled stack: measured motor thickness plus 3 mm tongue, 5 mm bridge and about 6 mm for nut and protrusion. Tighten evenly without crushing the motor case.
- **Propeller:** slide the prop onto the double-D shaft. Add the 2 mm spacer for P2-50, the 5 mm spacer for P3-50, both for P4-40, or neither for P3-60. Place the two collar halves around the shaft with their ridges engaged in the retaining groove. Install two M5 × 18 mm screws and hex nuts in opposite directions to distribute their weight. Snug them evenly. The groove carries axial retention and the shaft flats carry torque.

All four CAD propellers seated against the shoulder with zero volumetric interference at the checked orientation. The installed gears also cleared the closed case. Collar balance and retention under sustained load remain unmeasured. Turn the assembly by hand and check the collar before running it. The case is unsealed.
