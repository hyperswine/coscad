# Inner-frame partial print failure - owner report, 31 October 2026

The middle of Tesseract III (the inner frame from split Bed 2) has a partial
physical failure. The **lower diagonal female-threaded socket sections printed
poorly even though their supports were present and printed properly**, according
to the owner. The **upper diagonals printed well**. Lower/upper refer to the
original print orientation, not how the assembly is held in the photograph.

![Owner-supplied inner-frame photo](inner-frame-owner-photo.jpg)

The photo shows ragged/incomplete socket mouths and loose strands around
several bosses. It does not show supports in place or establish the initiating
failure mechanism. The report therefore does not establish missing/failed
support structures as the cause. Cause remains unknown; female-thread fit and
the other four hubs on Bed 2 have not been accepted by this report.

`outcome.json` preserves the owner observations, separate visual review, image
hash and the existing Bed 2 dispatch reference. No geometry, support settings,
G-code or printer controls were changed.
