# SG90 lead-screw lift

Run `sol build.sol` in this directory, then `coscad stl plate.coscad` to generate the four-part plate. The socket and 4.95 mm press-fit shaft bore follow the tested SG90 rack mount. The two-start screw advances 20 mm per turn. The cap and tower limit platform travel to about 10 mm.

Seat the servo with its cable toward the guide tower. Center it electrically, disconnect power and thread the screw into the platform. Slide the platform onto the guide rod and seat the screw on the servo with the platform near mid-travel. Press the cap onto the rectangular key while guiding the screw tip into the round bearing hole. Check free rotation and start with a centered ±30° command unloaded.

The P1S plate uses standard PLA, a 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.2 mm layers, four walls and 20% gyroid infill. The base and screw print upright.
